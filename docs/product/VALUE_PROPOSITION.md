# Value Proposition

TalentHub helps technical-professional students apply with better preparation and helps companies discover talent with verified context instead of relying only on generic resumes.

## For Students

- Understand why an opportunity fits or does not fit.
- Improve a profile before applying.
- Show evidence of real technical work.
- Use school validation as a trust signal.

## For Schools

- Validate competencies and evidence.
- Monitor readiness for internships or jobs.
- Mediate student-company interactions when required.

## For Companies

- Find candidates by specialty, skills and availability.
- Review evidence-backed profiles.
- Reduce noise in early talent screening.

## Differentiation

TalentHub is not just a labor social network. It is an assisted, verifiable application platform for technical-professional education.

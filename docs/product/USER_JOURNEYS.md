# User Journeys

## Visitor

1. Opens the landing page or public exploration.
2. Reviews only public student profiles, validated skills, public portfolios and freelance opportunities.
3. Signs in or registers as an Externo before publishing or responding.

## Estudiante

1. Receives an invitation or temporary account from a Colegio.
2. Signs in through the single login and changes the temporary password when required.
3. Completes profile, skills, portfolio and evidence.
4. Sends evidence for school review and sees its state.
5. Explores opportunities, reviews explainable compatibility and readiness recommendations.
6. Applies once per opportunity and follows the application timeline.
7. Uses mediated contact when the student is a minor.

## Empresa

1. Registers as an organization and verifies its email.
2. Completes company information and any institutional verification.
3. Publishes or manages its own corporate opportunities.
4. Searches authorized student profiles and reviews public evidence.
5. Manages applicants and proposes interviews.
6. Uses direct contact only with adults; minor contact requires school approval.

## Colegio

1. Enters through invitation, allowlist or approval.
2. Manages institution members according to their membership role.
3. Creates or imports students and assigns them to the institution.
4. Reviews evidence and validates skills for linked students.
5. Changes the student's academic stage without changing account type.
6. Reviews contact requests, internships and institutional metrics.

## Externo

1. Registers with a basic profile and verifies email.
2. Publishes a freelance opportunity with budget, modality, location and deadline.
3. Reviews proposals and communicates only through permitted privacy paths.
4. Cannot access company ATS or institutional administration.

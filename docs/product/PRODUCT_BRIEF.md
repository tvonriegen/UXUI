# Product Brief

TalentHub connects technical-professional students, graduates, schools and companies through verified profiles and assisted applications.

## Problem

Students often apply to internships without clear feedback about fit, missing evidence or missing institutional validation. Companies need a safer way to evaluate early technical talent, and schools need visibility into student readiness and employer interactions.

## Product Direction

TalentHub will prioritize three pillars:

- Explainable compatibility between students and opportunities.
- Student profiles strengthened with evidence, competencies and institutional validation.
- Assisted applications that guide students before they apply.

## Primary Users

- Student: builds a verified profile, uploads evidence and receives application guidance.
- Graduate: keeps institutional validation while gaining more direct employability tools.
- School: validates competencies, evidence and student readiness.
- Company: publishes opportunities and reviews compatible, verified talent.

## Current MVP

The current app already includes role-aware dashboards, talent search, profiles, jobs, messages, gamification, Supabase schema and demo seed data. The next work should turn these pieces into a coherent assisted application flow.
